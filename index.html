GET TO KNOW US

About Kings College Cotonou

A Tradition of Learning and Excellence

Kings College Cotonou is a private educational institution located in Cotonou, Benin Republic. Established on 23 September 2002, the school began its journey with just six pupils at its original site in Jericho, Cotonou.

Founded by Mr Solomon Ukaigwe, Kings College Cotonou has grown over the years into a learning community of more than 400 pupils and students, alongside its affiliated schools.

At Kings College Cotonou, we believe that education goes beyond academic performance. We are committed to the development of the whole child, recognising the importance of intellectual, physical, social and psychological growth.

Every child deserves the opportunity to learn, discover their abilities and build a strong foundation for their academic, personal and professional journey. Through quality education and the guidance of skilled instructors, we encourage our students to become responsible and impact-driven members of society.

Our Mission

To provide a world-class education that inspires students to achieve academic excellence, develop strong moral character, and become responsible global citizens.

Our Vision

To be the leading academic institution in West Africa, known for producing innovative thinkers and ethical leaders who shape the future of their communities.
